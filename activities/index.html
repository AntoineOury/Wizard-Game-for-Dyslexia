Hear the Letter, Tap the Letter

Activity Corpus ORT-17 · Hear, See Tap - Target + Distractors Model phase: ORT-15 · Hear, See, Tap - Single Target Letter Unit: Group 1 (lower-case) ≈ 1½ minutes

The tutor's exercise: letter cards on the table; "Point to the letter m." The child taps the letter they hear. A wrong tap is named, contrasted with the target by shape or by name, the right card is pointed out, the child taps it, and the letter comes back later in the drill.

How activities compose

Every activity is one phase of one corpus entry on 3–7 units, under two minutes, launched by link and reporting item-level results by postMessage (schema telemetry v1). It never decides mastery or what comes next; that belongs to the session layer. See activities/README.md for the contract, the build checklist and how to add the next activity.
